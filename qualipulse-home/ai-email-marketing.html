The problem

Why B2B SaaS Email Campaigns Stall Before the First Reply

Most SaaS teams can write a decent email. The trouble starts around it. A new domain sends at full volume on day one. An old list bounces. A sequence goes out without a spam check, and nobody owns deliverability, so nobody notices until the replies stop.

Most B2B email programs fail in the same three places.

The problem isn't the writing. It's everything around the email.

  • SymptomEmails land in spam, not the inboxA fresh domain sends too much too soon, or the main company domain takes the hit for a cold campaign.
    What we do insteadSecondary domains, warmed up gradually, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC in place before the first send.
  • SymptomLists bounce and complaints climbUnverified contacts and stale data push bounce rates up, and mailbox providers start filtering everything you send.
    What we do insteadEvery contact verified before it is sent to, and bounces removed as soon as they happen.
  • SymptomAI personalizes the first name, not the substanceMost AI powered email marketing stops at merge fields. The advice inside is the same for a CTO and a junior tester.
    What we do insteadAI picks the angle and the example for each role. Practitioners write the substance.

Guardrails

Guardrails That Protect Your Domain and Your Buyers

The biggest fear with AI email is a machine sending the wrong thing to a buyer you care about, or burning the domain your sales team depends on. These rules are part of every engagement. They are not an upgrade.

Consent and compliance

Every email says who sent it, uses an honest subject line, and carries a working unsubscribe link and your postal address.

We follow CAN-SPAM rules in the US (the FTC's CAN-SPAM compliance guide (opens in a new tab) is a good primer) and GDPR rules for contacts in the EU and UK. Your legal team still has the final word.

Human review of every email

No email sends without a person reading it first. AI drafts the variants and suggests the timing. A human approves the words, the facts and the offer.

Verified, lawful contacts only

Every contact is verified before every send. EU and UK contacts are emailed only where there is a lawful basis, and anyone who opts out is suppressed on every domain we run for you.

Sending limits that protect your domain

Every inbox has a daily cap. We watch bounce and complaint rates and pause a sequence if either climbs. Your main company domain never sends a campaign.

AI Email Marketing Questions

Straight answers to what B2B SaaS marketing leads ask us first.

Ask us something else →
What is AI email marketing?

AI email marketing uses AI to decide which email each contact gets, how it is framed and when it is sent, based on signals like role, industry and buying stage. At QualiPulse, AI handles the personalization and timing, practitioners write the content, and we manage everything around it: inboxes, warm-up, verification and deliverability.

What does Build, Operate, Transfer mean?

We build your email setup (domains, inboxes, DNS records, warm-up, a verified list and the sequences), then operate the campaigns for you: sending, monitoring and reporting. When your team is ready to run it, we transfer the domains, inboxes, sequences and playbook to you and train the people taking over. You are never locked in.

Do you set up the inboxes and domains?

Yes. We register secondary sending domains, create mailboxes on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, and configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC on each one. Everything is set up so it can move to your team at the transfer stage.

How long does warm-up take?

It depends on the domains, the mailbox provider and the volume you need, so we don't promise a fixed date. New inboxes typically need a few weeks of gradual warm-up before they reach full sending volume. Warm-up then keeps running in the background to protect reputation.

Where does the 15K contact list come from?

The list holds 15,000 verified B2B contacts, ready to use. It is built with B2B data tools such as Hunter.io and FullEnrich, matched to the roles and companies you sell to, and verified again before every send. Every contact is verified again before each send, unsubscribes are honored, US sends follow CAN-SPAM, and we only email EU and UK contacts where there is a lawful basis.

Will this hurt our main domain reputation?

No. Campaigns send from secondary domains, never from your main company domain, so your everyday email and sales conversations stay separate. We also warm up every inbox, cap daily sends and pause a sequence if bounces or complaints rise.

How do you track spam score and deliverability?

Every sequence gets a spam score check before launch, and we keep checking it while the campaign runs. We monitor inbox placement, bounce rate, complaint rate and domain reputation in Google Postmaster Tools, and keep spam complaints under the 0.1% Google recommends, well clear of the 0.3% threshold Gmail and Yahoo set for bulk senders.
