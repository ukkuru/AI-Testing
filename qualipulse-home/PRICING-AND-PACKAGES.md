# QualiPulse: services, keywords and pricing (USD, India-based delivery)

Prepared November 2026. Market rates are from published 2026 pricing (sources at the end). Keyword volumes are US monthly searches from DataForSEO. KD is keyword difficulty, 0 to 100.

## The pricing position in one line

Price at roughly 40 to 60% of a US specialist agency, and well above a generic Indian content shop. You sell practitioner depth from India, not cheap writing from India.

### Why your current prices are too low

| Current package | What it includes | Effective price |
|---|---|---|
| Content Growth, $699/month | 5 blogs of 1,500 words, 2 infographics each, on-page SEO, keyword research, 10 hours of consulting | About $140 per article, with design and consulting thrown in |
| Content and Distribution, $999/month | 7 blogs, 1 case study, 1 whitepaper | About $90 per piece |

- **The benchmark.** Freelance B2B SaaS writers charge $0.25 to $0.45 a word, which is $375 to $675 for a 1,500-word article. The median published SEO content package is $2,500 a month.
- **The risk.** At $140 an article, a US buyer files you with the generic content mills your own homepage criticises. A low price undercuts the practitioner claim.
- **Indian SEO firms.** 76% charge $1,000 a month or less (Ahrefs). Above that line, you are clearly in a different tier, and you need to be.
- **Existing clients.** Keep current clients on their price for 3 to 6 months, then move them up. Apply new prices to new clients immediately.

## Pricing page: content and keywords

- **Searches for pricing pages are tiny.** "content marketing pricing" gets 20 a month, and "content writing services pricing" and "blog writing service pricing" get 10 each. The pricing page exists to convert visitors, not to rank.
- **Two existing posts already target pricing searches:** /seo-pricing/ and /content-writing-services-pricing/. Link them from the pricing page.

Suggested structure:
1. **Headline:** "Practitioner-led content at a fair price." One line on why you cost more than content mills and less than US agencies.
2. **Tabs, one per service,** with the packages above. Mark "Most popular" on one package per service.
3. **"What is always included":** practitioner review, keyword research, on-page SEO, reporting, and you own everything.
4. **Pass-through costs and terms:** minimums, notice period, payment terms. State the 30-day notice period here if it stands.
5. **Comparison strip:** "Generic content mill vs US agency vs QualiPulse", covering price, who writes, technical review and turnaround.
6. **Pricing FAQ** (6 questions): why the minimum, can I pause, what currency, do you sign NDAs, what if a piece misses the mark, are tools included.
7. **One clear call to action:** "Book a 30-minute call" (cal.com) and "Get a free content plan".
