The Situation

A Platform With Real Proof and No System to Compound It

This was not a company starting from zero. The platform had credible partnerships with national and international sports federations, a full year of steady LinkedIn posting, hundreds of Instagram posts, and a genuinely well-designed website. The content, the design talent, and the real-world proof points already existed.

What was missing was a system connecting them. Each channel was being run and measured on its own, with no shared proof engine feeding all of them from the same evidence, and no way to tell which of the existing wins were actually worth repeating.

Strength

The Best Content Already Worked

One specific LinkedIn post format, a named pain point paired with a specific number, consistently outperformed every generic post on the same page

Strength

Real Proof, When Shown, Performed

Instagram posts featuring real people or partner tags pulled 6 to 25 times the account's own baseline engagement, but this only happened twice in six months

Strength

Operational Discipline Was Already There

Close to 280 Instagram posts and a full year of consistent LinkedIn posting showed the execution muscle existed, it just needed better direction

Root Cause Analysis

Four Channels, Five Symptoms, One Root Cause

A marketing consultant looking at any one of these channels alone would have recommended a channel-specific fix. Looked at together, a different pattern emerged: this was not four separate content problems, it was one missing proof infrastructure showing up five different ways.

LinkedIn

Real content existed, but proof did not compound: only 1 testimonial appeared across 38 posts.

Instagram

Real moments outperformed everything else by 6 to 25 times, but happened only twice in six months.

Website

Two testimonials existed on the site, but no case study, video, or named result stood behind either one.

AI Search Visibility

The platforms AI answer engines cite most, including Reddit, Quora, and Wikipedia, knew almost nothing about the company.

The pattern

Cross-Channel Pattern

Every channel had proof somewhere. No channel had a system feeding that proof to the others.

A company with a genuine content weakness needs different fixes per channel. A company with a systemic proof gap needs one system, built once, that feeds every channel from the same source.

What Was Delivered

Five Pillars, Sequenced Deliberately

The strategy was sequenced on purpose. Foundation and proof come before content and new features, because a viral post pointing at a broken site, or a proof engine with no story to feed it, wastes the effort behind it.

01

Fix the Foundation

  • Technical SEO cleanup
  • Sitemap and federation subdomain fixes
  • Disavowing spam backlinks
02

Build a Proof Engine

  • Case studies and reviews, systematized once
  • Reused consistently across every channel
03

Speak to the Right Buyer

  • LinkedIn rebuilt around academy and federation buyers
  • Instagram rebuilt around players and culture
04

Show Up Where AI Looks

  • Reddit and Quora presence built from scratch
  • Structured content and an llms.txt file shipped
05

Turn Users Into the Engine

  • Specced an in-product feature that auto-generates a shareable, branded moment the instant something worth sharing happens
How We Worked

A Phased Roadmap, Not a Single Big Launch

Each phase was scoped so the client had a working result before the next phase began, rather than one large deliverable at the end of the engagement.

  1. 1Weeks 1 to 4

    Foundation

    Fix site duplication, submit federation sitemaps, disavow spam links, ship llms.txt.

  2. 2Weeks 3 to 8

    Proof Engine

    Real case studies, claim review platforms, spec the in-product sharing feature.

  3. 3Weeks 6 to 12

    Channel Activation

    Rebuild the LinkedIn and Instagram content mix, launch Reddit and Quora presence.

  4. 4Month 4+

    Compounding

    Full feature rollout, press outreach, paid acquisition once proof exists.
