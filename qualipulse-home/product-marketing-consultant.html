The problem

Why Product Value Breaks Across Organizational Boundaries

Most reviews look at one function at a time: a marketing audit, a usability study, a security scan. Customers experience the whole chain at once.

That is exactly where a product marketing consultant working alone, or a QA team working alone, misses the real story.

The result is value leakage: a gap between what the market expects and what the product delivers.

  • Where it leaksRoadmaps without market evidenceFeatures get prioritized on internal opinion, not on what buyers will pay for.
    Pillar that catches itMarket Relevance
  • Where it leaksClaims that run ahead of proofCampaigns promise more than the product, the case studies, or the test results can back up.
    Pillar that catches itMarket Influence
  • Where it leaksFunnels that underperformThe messaging does not match buyer reality, so demos and trials stall.
    Pillar that catches itDemand Performance
  • Where it leaksTrust that erodes after the saleOnboarding, usability, reliability, and security gaps turn new customers into support tickets.
    Pillar that catches itExperience
The framework

Four Pillars, One Evidence Standard

Each pillar is assessed the same way: alignment, evidence, execution, and business value. That shared standard is what makes this product marketing consulting work comparable across marketing and engineering.

Pillar 1

Market Relevance

Right problem, right buyer, evidence-based roadmap. We check whether what you build lines up with what your market actually needs.

Pillar 2

Market Influence

Positioning, SEO, content, and authority. How clearly the market hears your story, and whether the story holds up.

Pillar 3

Demand Performance

Campaigns, conversion, demos, and trials. Where interest turns into pipeline, and where it quietly drops off.

Pillar 4

Experience

Two scored dimensions: usability and value delivery, plus technical reliability (performance, security).

Scoring

The Product Value Maturity Model

A single headline score would hide the real constraints. So we use four pillars and five scored dimensions, each rated 1 to 5.

  1. 1DisconnectedFunctions work separately. Claims and priorities conflict.
  2. 2CoordinatedTeams share plans, but measures remain activity based.
  3. 3IntegratedShared journeys connect product, marketing, and quality.
  4. 4Evidence LedCustomer, funnel, and product data guide decisions.
  5. 5Value AssuredCritical promises are measured continuously.

The five scored dimensions

Illustrative profile, not client data
  • Market Relevance
  • Market Influence
  • Demand Performance
  • Experience: usability and value delivery
  • Experience: technical reliability

Target maturity reflects business need. Not every company needs Level 5 in every pillar. A seed-stage tool might aim for Level 3 on reliability and Level 4 on relevance, and that can be the right call.

Who it is for

Marketing blames the product, engineering blames the leads, and sales blames the pricing. A shared, scored view settles the argument with evidence.

Trials start but do not convert

Often the gap is not the message or the product alone. It is the space between them, which is where a product value assurance review looks first.

What is Product Value Assurance?

Product Value Assurance is a founder-led assessment that scores a technology product across four pillars: Market Relevance, Market Influence, Demand Performance, and Experience, which covers both usability and technical reliability. It connects marketing claims to product and engineering evidence in one engagement.

How is this different from a marketing audit or a QA audit alone?

A product marketing consultant rarely tests the technical evidence behind a claim, and a QA or testing firm rarely connects product findings to market positioning and commercial performance. This service is led by practitioners who can challenge both the promise and the product behind it, in the same engagement.

What does the Executive Diagnostic include?

One product, one market, and up to three critical customer journeys, scored across all four pillars and five dimensions at the same evidence-based depth: interviews, documentation review, a live product walkthrough, usability and onboarding analytics, and available performance and security documentation.

Do you test reliability, performance, and security directly?

Yes, in the Deep-Dive Modules stage once the Executive Diagnostic flags that pillar for further investment. This can include live load testing, security review, and automation assessment, delivered directly by QualiPulse or co-branded with a named, credentialed partner where a compliance-grade certificate is required.

What size company is this suited for?

This works well for founder-led product teams, roughly five to fifty people, that already have a real product and some traction but no dedicated function connecting product, marketing, and quality evidence yet. Later-stage companies typically start with a Deep-Dive Module on their weakest pillar instead.

How long does the assessment take?

The Executive Diagnostic is scoped around one product, one market, and up to three customer journeys, and is typically completed in a matter of weeks. Deep-Dive Modules run separately once the diagnostic identifies which pillar needs further investment.

Will our product and company details stay confidential?

Yes. Client names, product names, and proprietary details are never published without written approval, and any case study describing the work is reviewed and anonymized where needed before it goes live.
