## Pricing Questions

More answers on the [FAQ page](https://qualipulse.tech/faq/).

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans need 30 days of written notice to cancel. There are no long-term contracts and no cancellation penalties. We earn the next month through results.

Why are there no one-off packages?

Search authority, LinkedIn visibility and AI citations build over months, not in a single delivery. Monthly plans let us plan topics ahead, measure what works and adjust, which a one-off project cannot do.

What currency are the prices in?

All prices are in US dollars. Monthly plans are billed each month; Career Positioning is a one-time fee per person.

Are tools included?

AI Email Marketing is the only plan with pass-through costs: sending tools, inboxes, domains and data credits are billed at cost, and we agree them with you before anything is bought.

How long before we see results?

Most clients see movement in search rankings between 60 and 90 days. Content authority compounds, and the bigger returns usually show at the four to six month mark, which is why our plans are monthly.

Who actually creates the content?

Software practitioners with real industry experience, not junior writers with a research brief. Every piece is reviewed before delivery by someone who has done the kind of work it describes.

Do you offer a sample before we commit?

Yes. Ask for a sample through the contact form and we will share work that matches your market and audience before you decide anything.

What if no plan fits?

Tell us what you need. If your volume, audience or channels do not map to a standard plan, we will scope a custom engagement and quote it as a fixed monthly price.
