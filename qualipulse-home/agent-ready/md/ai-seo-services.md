## AI SEO vs Traditional SEO

You will see this work sold as AI search optimization, answer engine optimization services or generative engine optimization services. Different labels, same job: make your site easy to find, easy to understand and easy to quote.

| Area | Traditional SEO | AI SEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goal | Rank a page in Google's results | Get your brand named and cited inside AI answers |

What are AI SEO services?

AI SEO services make your website easy for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and cite. The work combines technical SEO, schema markup, AI crawler access, answer-ready content and brand mentions, then tracks how often AI search names you.

How is an AI SEO agency different from a regular SEO agency?

A regular SEO agency works to rank your pages in Google's results. An AI SEO agency also works to get your brand quoted inside AI answers. That adds prompt testing, AI crawler checks, schema, entity building and citation tracking on top of classic SEO.

How long until AI search cites us?

There is no fixed date, and anyone who gives you one is guessing. Technical fixes can show up once your pages are recrawled. Citations take longer because they depend on your authority and on how crowded your topic is. We report what changes every month so you can judge progress for yourself.

Do you guarantee AI citations?

No. Nobody controls what ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google choose to cite, so no honest agency can guarantee it. What we can promise is the work: a clean technical base, pages worth quoting and monthly tracking that shows exactly where you stand.

What is answer engine optimization?

Answer engine optimization (AEO) means shaping your content so AI tools and search features can lift a direct answer from it. In practice: a clear question, a short answer up top, supporting detail below and structured data. It is one part of our AI SEO services.

Do I still need traditional SEO?

Yes. AI search tools lean on pages that are already crawlable, indexed and trusted, and Google's AI Overviews only draw on pages in its index. If your technical SEO is weak, AI SEO has nothing to build on.

When should you hire an AEO consultant?

Hire an AEO consultant when AI tools name your competitors and not you, and nobody on your team has the time or the know-how to fix it. Other good triggers are a product launch, a move into a new category, organic leads dropping while rankings hold steady, or a tracking tool full of data nobody acts on. If you already have an SEO lead and writers with spare capacity, a self-serve tool may be enough to start.
