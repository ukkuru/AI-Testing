The gap

## The Gap Between Your Experience and How You Are Presenting It

You have built critical systems. You have led organizational transformation. You have managed teams, influenced leadership, and shaped company direction. Your resume says you "managed process improvements" and "led a team of five."

The gap matters. Hiring decision-makers do not read your full story. They scan for one signal: can you operate at the level we need? If that signal is missing in ten seconds, they move to the next candidate.

Your experience is not the problem. The way it is written is.

- Symptom**Your resume lists duties, not results**"Responsible for test automation" tells a reader what your job description said. It says nothing about what you changed.

  What we do insteadWe dig out the outcome behind each role and put it first.
- Symptom**Your LinkedIn headline is just your job title**Recruiters search for the level they are hiring for. A headline that only repeats your current title rarely tells them you are ready for the next one.

  What we do insteadA headline and About section written for the role you want next.
- Symptom**You keep hearing "overqualified"**Often that means your story is unclear. The reader cannot see where you are heading, so they guess, and they guess wrong.

  What we do insteadOne clear career narrative that runs through your resume, your profile and your interviews.

What is career positioning for software engineers?

Career positioning means presenting your experience so it matches the level of role you want. For software engineers that usually means a resume built on outcomes, a LinkedIn profile written for your next role, and one clear story you can tell in interviews.

Do you rewrite my resume from scratch?

We start from your current resume and your own account of the work, then rewrite it. Very little of the original wording usually survives, but every fact comes from you. We never add achievements you did not have.

Do you also rewrite my LinkedIn profile?

Yes. Your headline, About section and experience entries are part of the service. Recruiters often see your LinkedIn profile before your resume, so the two need to tell the same story.

Is this only for developers?

No. We work with senior software engineers, QA and test leads, engineering managers, architects and independent consultants. If you have spent years in software and want a senior, lead or director-level role, it fits.

Do you guarantee interviews or a job offer?

No. Nobody can honestly promise that, because hiring decisions depend on the market, the company and you. What we promise is a resume and profile that show your real level clearly, so your experience gets a fair reading.
