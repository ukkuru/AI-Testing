FAQ

Your questions, answered before the call.

The questions CIOs, CTOs, Chief AI Officers, and AI Heads ask before they book a walkthrough.

What is a digital adoption platform, and what does Cognior add to it?

A digital adoption platform guides people inside the software they already use. Product Fluency does that job. Cognior adds the usage data, license records, and AI and cloud controls around it, so guidance, renewal decisions, and AI policy all rest on the same evidence.

See the four products →

How do I reduce unused software licenses?

Start by matching license records against real usage, not just logins. Intelligent Sights compares entitlements from software asset management with activity inside each application. Seats with no meaningful activity over a window you set get flagged for reclaim before renewal. Applications with low use and high friction get guidance first, so you don't cut a tool people need.

See how Intelligent Sights flags unused licenses →

How do I govern employee use of AI tools, including shadow AI?

ETHOS places a gateway in front of your models. Its guardrails run in under 12 milliseconds, block prompt injection, and tokenize sensitive data before it leaves your perimeter. Every request is logged with the user, department, and policy that applied. Intelligent Sights finds shadow AI tools in use, so teams can move that work onto approved options.

See how ETHOS governs AI requests →

How do I measure AI copilot productivity?

Measure each request from prompt to outcome, not from the number of licenses issued. Intelligent Sights shows whether the task finished, whether the person accepted or overrode the output, and whether someone bypassed the tool for an unapproved one. Run a pilot on your own workflows before you set a productivity target.

See how AI copilot ROI is measured →

How quickly can an enterprise deploy Cognior?

A browser extension covers third-party apps such as Salesforce, SAP, Workday, and ServiceNow. A lightweight SDK covers software you build. Read only connectors cover Azure subscriptions, and your first dependency graph is ready in under 15 minutes. Telemetry is masked before it leaves the browser, and controls map to GDPR and SOC 2.
