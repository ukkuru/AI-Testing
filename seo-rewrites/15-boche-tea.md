## How to spot fake lucky draw sites

Searches like "boche tea lucky draw website" and "boche tea contact number" are exactly what scammers target. Watch for these signs:

* **A slightly different web address.** Extra words, hyphens or odd endings (for example a .xyz or .online domain) instead of the address on the brand's verified social pages.
* **"Pay a fee to claim your prize."** Real prize promotions do not ask winners to pay tax, processing or delivery fees first.
* **Requests for OTPs, PINs or card numbers.** No genuine company needs your OTP to give you money.
* **Personal numbers and UPI IDs.** Payments to an individual's account are a red flag.
* **Numbers from comments or forwards.** Take contact details only from the official website or verified pages.

If you have lost money or shared bank details, call the national cyber fraud helpline **1930** quickly, or file a complaint at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in/). Fast reporting improves the chance of freezing the money.
