## What is Mint Majesty tea?

Mint Majesty is one of the Teavana herbal blends served as a hot brewed tea at Starbucks. It is not a "real" tea in the botanical sense, because it contains no leaves from the tea plant (Camellia sinensis). It is an herbal infusion, sometimes called a tisane.

### What is in Mint Majesty tea?

The blend has three ingredients:

* **Spearmint:** sweet, soft and rounded
* **Peppermint:** sharper and cooler, thanks to its high menthol content
* **Lemon verbena:** a lemony herb that adds brightness without any sourness

That is it. No black or green tea, no sweetener and no flavouring syrups in the base tea.

### Does Mint Majesty have caffeine?

No. Mint Majesty is caffeine free, because none of its three herbs contain caffeine. This is one of the main reasons people order it in the evening or swap it in for a second coffee.

Be careful not to confuse it with Teavana Jade Citrus Mint, which is a green tea blend and does contain caffeine.

## What does Mint Majesty tea taste like?

Think of a fresh mint leaf you have just rubbed between your fingers, then add a squeeze of lemon peel.

* **First sip:** a cool peppermint rush that opens up your nose
* **Middle:** spearmint softens it and adds a gentle natural sweetness
* **Finish:** lemon verbena leaves a light, clean, citrus note

It is not bitter and not sweet, and the body is light. It tastes more refreshing and less medicinal than plain peppermint tea, because the spearmint and lemon verbena round off the sharp edges.

If you find it too mild, steep it longer or ask for an extra bag. If you find it too strong, take the bag out after three minutes.

## Is Mint Majesty tea good for you?

For most people, yes. It is a caffeine free, calorie free drink made only from herbs, so it is a far better habit than sugary drinks. Just keep expectations realistic: it is a pleasant, healthy drink, not a medicine.

### Digestion

This is the benefit with the most support. Peppermint oil has been studied for irritable bowel syndrome, with several trials finding it eases cramping and bloating. A cup of tea contains far less oil than a capsule, so the effect is gentler, but a warm mint tea after a heavy meal is a long standing habit for a reason.

### Hydration with zero calories

A grande Mint Majesty has no calories and no sugar when you order it plain. If you struggle to drink enough water, a hot herbal tea is an easy way to add fluid. We cover this in more detail in our guide on [whether herbal tea counts toward your daily water intake](https://teastory.blog/2025/11/02/does-herbal-tea-count-toward-your-daily-water-intake/).

### Calm and sleep

There is no strong proof that mint itself relaxes you. The real win is what it replaces. Swapping an evening coffee or black tea for a caffeine free cup can help you fall asleep more easily.

### Who should be careful

* **Acid reflux or heartburn:** peppermint can relax the valve at the top of the stomach and make reflux worse for some people. If you notice this, switch to a spearmint only tea or skip it.
* **Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** mint tea in normal amounts is commonly considered fine, but check with your doctor or midwife, especially about drinking several cups a day.
* **Medications:** if you take regular medication, ask your pharmacist before drinking large amounts of any herbal tea.

## How to order Mint Majesty at Starbucks

Ask for a hot Mint Majesty tea in your size. Starbucks hot teas are usually made with one tea bag in a tall and two in a grande or venti, topped with hot water.

Easy ways to make it your own:

* **Add honey** if you want it slightly sweet
* **Add a lemon slice or a splash of lemonade** for more citrus
* **Ask for it iced** if your store will do it; some will brew it and pour it over ice
* **Ask for an extra bag** for a stronger mint flavour

## How to make Mint Majesty tea at home

You can buy Teavana Mint Majesty tea bags in many grocery stores, though stock varies by country. Or blend your own.

### Using a tea bag

1. Boil fresh water. Herbal teas like mint are fine with fully boiling water.
2. Pour about 240 ml (8 oz) over one bag.
3. Cover the cup and steep for 5 minutes. Covering keeps the mint oils in your cup.
4. Remove the bag. Add honey or lemon if you like.

### Copycat loose leaf blend

Mix these dried herbs in a jar:

* 2 parts spearmint
* 1 part peppermint
* 1 part lemon verbena

Use about 1 heaped teaspoon per cup and steep for 5 minutes in boiling water. Adjust the peppermint up for more bite or down for a softer cup. Store the blend in an airtight jar away from light and use it within a few months for the best aroma.

### Iced Mint Majesty

Brew two bags in 240 ml of boiling water for 5 minutes, then pour over a full glass of ice. Add a sprig of fresh mint and a lemon wedge. On a hot day it is one of the most refreshing caffeine free drinks you can make.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Mint Majesty tea?
Mint Majesty is a Teavana herbal tea sold at Starbucks. It is a caffeine free blend of spearmint, peppermint and lemon verbena.

### What does Mint Majesty tea taste like?
It tastes cool and fresh from the peppermint, gently sweet from the spearmint, and finishes with a light lemon note from the lemon verbena. It is not bitter.

### Does Mint Majesty tea have caffeine?
No. All three ingredients are herbs with no caffeine, so you can drink it at any time of day.

### Is Mint Majesty tea good for you?
Yes, for most people. It has no caffeine, calories or sugar, it adds to your fluid intake, and peppermint may help with bloating. People with acid reflux may find peppermint makes it worse.

### What are the benefits of Mint Majesty tea?
The main benefits are gentle digestive comfort from peppermint, hydration without calories, fresh breath, and an easy caffeine free swap for evening coffee.

### Is Mint Majesty the same as peppermint tea?
No. It contains peppermint, but also spearmint and lemon verbena, which make it softer and slightly lemony compared with straight peppermint tea.

### How many calories are in Mint Majesty tea?
Zero, when served plain. Adding honey or lemonade adds calories and sugar.

## The bottom line

Mint Majesty is a simple, honest drink: spearmint, peppermint and lemon verbena, with no caffeine and no calories. It tastes clean and refreshing, it is good for you as an everyday swap for sugary or caffeinated drinks, and peppermint may ease an unsettled stomach. Order it hot at Starbucks, ask for honey if you like it sweet, or mix your own blend at home for a few cents a cup.
