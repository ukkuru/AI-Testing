## What is a non alcoholic malt beverage?

Malt starts as grain, usually barley. The grain is soaked until it sprouts, which wakes up enzymes that turn starch into sugar. Then it is dried and toasted. That toasted grain is malt, and it gives these drinks their bready, caramel flavour.

To make bottled malta, brewers mash the malt with water and hops, just like the first stage of beer. The difference is the next step. Beer is fermented with yeast so the sugars turn into alcohol. Malta skips that step or stops it almost immediately, so the sugar stays in the bottle. The drink is then sweetened, often coloured with caramel, and carbonated.

In most countries a drink can be labelled non alcoholic at up to 0.5% ABV. Many maltas sit well below that. If you avoid alcohol completely for religious or medical reasons, check the label of your specific brand.

### Types of malt drinks

| Type | Examples | Fizzy? | Caffeine? |
| - | - | - | - |
| Carbonated malta | Malta Guinness, Maltina, Malta Goya, Malta India, Vita Malt | Yes | No |
| Flavoured malt beverage | Barbican (apple, lemon, strawberry and other flavours) | Yes | No |

| Coffee malt drink | Malted coffee mixes and "coffee malt" blends | Sometimes | Yes, from the coffee |

The rule of thumb: malt itself contains no caffeine. Caffeine only shows up when a brand adds cocoa, coffee or tea.

## Does malta have caffeine?

No. Malted barley does not contain caffeine, and caffeine is not a listed ingredient in standard malta. Here is how the brands people ask about most line up.

* **Malta Guinness:** no caffeine. The UK label lists carbonated water, sugar, sorghum, barley malt, caramel, hops, a stabiliser and added vitamins. No caffeine, no milk.
* **Maltina:** a carbonated malt drink with no added caffeine in its standard version.
* **Vita Malt (Vitamalt):** no caffeine in the classic malt flavour.
* **Malta Goya and Malta India:** Caribbean and Latin American style maltas, also without added caffeine.
* **Barbican:** a flavoured malt beverage, no caffeine.

Two caveats. Recipes vary by country, and some brands sell energy or "plus" versions. If the can says energy, coffee or cola on the front, read the ingredients. And if you are very sensitive to caffeine, the label always beats any blog post, including this one.

Compare that with your usual cuppa. A cup of black tea has roughly 40 to 50 mg of caffeine. If you are reducing caffeine but want something warm instead, our guide to [caffeine free herbal teas](https://teastory.blog/2024/05/02/how-different-herbal-tea-varieties-can-boost-your-health/) is a good place to start, and you can see [how much caffeine is in iced tea](https://teastory.blog/2025/11/11/iced-tea-caffeine-content-what-you-need-to-know/) if that is your summer habit.

## Is malt a carbonated drink?

Bottled and canned malta is carbonated. It is fizzy, served cold, and pours with a foamy head that makes it look like stout. So yes, it has gas, and it counts as a soft drink in most supermarkets.

Malt powders like Horlicks and Ovaltine are not carbonated. You stir them into hot milk or water, so there are no bubbles at all.

The fizz matters for two reasons. First, carbonated drinks can make you feel bloated if you have a sensitive stomach. Second, the bubbles and cold temperature mask how sweet malta is, which makes it easy to drink a whole bottle without noticing the sugar.

| Malta Guinness | about 57 kcal | about 11 g | None |
| Regular cola | about 42 kcal | about 10.6 g | about 10 mg |

Malta Guinness figures are from the UK retail label. Values vary by country and recipe.

A 330 ml bottle of Malta Guinness carries roughly 36 g of sugar. That is more than the daily free sugar limit the NHS suggests for adults (30 g) in a single bottle. Other maltas are in the same range. So malta is not a low sugar alternative to cola; it sits right alongside it.

Some brands now sell lighter or no sugar versions. If you drink malta often, those are worth looking for.

## Non alcoholic malt drink benefits: what holds up

Malta is not a superfood, but it does have real upsides when you drink it for the right reasons.

### Quick energy without caffeine

Does malta give you energy? Yes, from sugar and carbohydrates. That makes it useful after a long day in the heat, after sport, or when you need a lift but cannot have caffeine. It is not an energy drink in the stimulant sense. The lift is fuel, not a buzz, and it fades the way any sugar boost does.

### An alcohol free option that still feels like a "proper" drink

This is the real reason most people drink malta. It looks and tastes like a dark beer, so it works at parties, weddings and family meals where you want something grown up without the alcohol.

### Some added vitamins

Many maltas, including Malta Guinness, add B vitamins. Malted grain also carries small amounts of B vitamins naturally. That is a nice extra, but a bottle of malta is not a meaningful vitamin source compared with a normal varied diet.

## Brand by brand: is it healthy?

### Is Vita Malt healthy?

Vita Malt is a classic carbonated malt drink with no caffeine and no alcohol. Like other maltas it is high in sugar, so it is fine as an occasional drink but not something to have every day. People like it for the quick energy and the familiar taste.

### Maltina drink benefits

Maltina gives you the same things as other maltas: caffeine free energy from sugar, a few added vitamins, and an alcohol free drink for social occasions. The trade off is the same too: plenty of sugar per bottle.

### Malta Guinness benefits

Malta Guinness has no caffeine, no alcohol, no milk and added vitamins. It is one of the sweeter maltas at roughly 36 g of sugar per 330 ml, so treat it as a treat.

### Barbican malt drink benefits

Barbican is a lighter tasting, flavoured malt beverage popular in the Middle East. It is fizzy and caffeine free, and some flavours are less heavy than a dark malta. Check the label for sugar, since it varies by flavour.

### Malta India drink benefits

Malta India is a Caribbean style malta with the same profile: no caffeine, no alcohol, sweet and carbonated. Fine as an occasional drink.

## Malt drink side effects and who should go easy

* **Blood sugar:** the high sugar load can spike blood glucose. If you have diabetes or prediabetes, malta is not a good regular choice.
* **Teeth:** sugar plus carbonation is hard on enamel. Drink it with a meal rather than sipping all afternoon.
* **Weight:** at around 190 calories a bottle, two or three a day adds up fast.
* **Gluten:** malt comes from barley, so malt drinks are not gluten free and are unsuitable for people with coeliac disease.
* **Pregnancy and children:** malta has no caffeine or meaningful alcohol, but the sugar is still the issue. Keep portions small, and ask your doctor or midwife if you are unsure about trace alcohol.

## Malt drink vs tea: which should you reach for?

If you want alertness, tea wins with its mix of caffeine and L theanine. If you want something with almost no calories and plenty of plant compounds, unsweetened tea wins again (see the [health benefits of different types of tea](https://teastory.blog/2024/05/13/the-health-benefits-of-different-types-of-tea/)).

If you want a cold, sweet, fizzy drink with no caffeine and no alcohol, malta is a better fit than either. And for a calm, caffeine free evening drink without the sugar, a cup of [chamomile tea](https://teastory.blog/2025/10/13/chamomile-in-tamil-meaning-benefits-and-tea-preparation/) does the job with almost zero calories.

## FAQ

### Does malt have caffeine?
No. Malt is made from sprouted, dried barley, and barley does not contain caffeine. Caffeine only appears in malt drinks that add cocoa, coffee or tea, such as chocolate malt powders or coffee malt blends.

### Does Malta Guinness contain caffeine?
No. Malta Guinness lists carbonated water, sugar, sorghum, barley malt, caramel, hops, a stabiliser and vitamins. There is no caffeine on the ingredient list.

### Does Malta Guinness contain milk?
No. Malta Guinness is a carbonated malt drink with no milk or dairy ingredients listed. Malted milk powders like Horlicks are a different product.

### Is malt a carbonated drink?
Bottled and canned malta is carbonated, so it is fizzy like a soft drink. Malt powders mixed with milk or water are not carbonated.

### Does malt contain alcohol?
Non alcoholic malt drinks are brewed without the fermentation step that makes alcohol, or with it stopped early. They are labelled under 0.5% ABV and many contain far less.

### Is malt an energy drink?
Not in the usual sense. It has no caffeine or stimulants. It gives you energy through sugar and carbohydrates, which is a gentler lift than a caffeinated energy drink.

### Is Vita Malt good for you?
Vita Malt is caffeine free and alcohol free with some added vitamins, but it is high in sugar. It is fine as an occasional drink, not as a daily health drink.

## Bottom line

A non alcoholic malt drink gives you the look and taste of a dark beer with no alcohol and no caffeine. Malta Guinness, Maltina, Vita Malt, Malta Goya, Malta India and Barbican all fit that description. Bottled malta is fizzy, and it carries about as much sugar as cola. Enjoy it as a treat or a quick energy boost. For something you can drink every day, unsweetened tea is the better choice.
