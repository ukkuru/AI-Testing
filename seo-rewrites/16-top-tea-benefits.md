## What is Top Tea and who makes it?

Top Tea is a tea bag brand made by Promasidor, the company behind several everyday food brands sold across Africa. In Nigeria it is sold by Promasidor Nigeria, and it is one of the most common tea bags on shop shelves and in market kiosks across the country.

According to the official Top Tea website, every variety is a blend of "100% pure black teas" from the Camellia sinensis plant, sourced from tea estates around the world. The range comes in three flavours:

* **Classic (traditional black tea)**, in big round bags and in tagged bags
* **Lemon and Lime**, black tea with a citrus flavour
* **Ginger**, black tea with a ginger flavour

There is no green tea or herbal version in the official range. So every Top Tea bag, including the ginger and lemon ones, contains caffeine, and the benefits of Top Tea are the benefits of black tea. The brand also says one round bag makes two cups.

## Does Top Tea contain caffeine?

Yes. Top Tea is black tea, and all black tea contains caffeine unless it has been decaffeinated. The Top Tea product page states "natural mild caffeine content: < 50mg per cup" and elsewhere says "50mg of natural mild caffeine per cup".

That figure is in line with independent data. The Mayo Clinic puts a regular 8 oz (237 ml) cup of brewed black tea at about 47 to 48 mg of caffeine, against roughly 95 mg for the same cup of brewed coffee.

| Drink (about 237 ml) | Typical caffeine |
|-|-|
| Top Tea, one cup (brand figure) | under 50 mg |
| Brewed black tea (Mayo Clinic) | about 47 to 48 mg |
| Brewed coffee (Mayo Clinic) | about 95 mg |
| Decaf black tea | about 2 mg |
| Herbal tea such as hibiscus (zobo) or peppermint | 0 mg |

Two things change the number in your cup:

* **Steeping time and strength.** A bag left in the cup for five minutes gives more caffeine than one dipped for a minute.
* **One bag, one cup.** The round bag is designed for two cups. If you brew one big round bag in a single mug, expect more than the stated 50 mg.

The Ginger and Lemon and Lime flavours have about the same caffeine as Classic, since the tea leaves are the same. We go into bottled and iced teas in our guide to [how much caffeine is in iced tea](https://teastory.blog/2025/11/11/iced-tea-caffeine-content-what-you-need-to-know/).

### How much Top Tea is too much?

The US FDA says up to 400 mg of caffeine a day is generally not linked to harmful effects in healthy adults. At under 50 mg a cup, that is several cups of Top Tea, as long as you are not also drinking coffee, energy drinks or cola. During pregnancy the usual advice is to stay under 200 mg a day from all sources. Children and people who are sensitive to caffeine should have less.

## Benefits of Top Tea in the body

Here are the health benefits of Top Tea you can reasonably expect, based on what black tea contains and what research shows.

### 1. A gentle lift in energy and focus

Caffeine is the main active ingredient. At around 50 mg a cup, it is enough to make you feel more alert without the stronger jolt of coffee. Tea also contains theanine, an amino acid that the brand highlights. Small studies suggest theanine and caffeine together may help attention, though the effect from one cup is modest.

### 2. Plant antioxidants

Black tea is rich in flavonoids, including theaflavins and thearubigins that form when the leaf is oxidised. These protect cells from damage in lab studies. How much they do inside the body from everyday drinking is still being studied.

### 3. Possible support for heart health

The strongest human evidence comes from large observational studies. A 2022 study by the US National Cancer Institute followed nearly half a million adults in the UK Biobank for about 11 years. People who drank two or more cups of tea a day, mostly black tea, had a 9 to 13 percent lower risk of death from any cause than non tea drinkers, and lower risk of death from heart disease and stroke ([NIH summary](https://nihrecord.nih.gov/2022/09/16/nih-study-suggests-health-benefits-black-tea)). This shows a link, not proof that tea caused it, and it applied whether people took their tea with milk or sugar.

### 4. Fluid with almost no calories

A cup of plain Top Tea is mostly water and has only a couple of calories. It counts toward your daily fluids. The mild caffeine does not cancel that out at normal amounts.

### 5. Ginger flavour for a settled stomach?

Ginger has some evidence for easing nausea, but the brand describes Top Tea Ginger as tea "with a healthy ginger zing" and does not say how much ginger it contains. Treat it as a flavour, not a remedy. For a stronger ginger effect, add slices of fresh ginger to the pot.

For a wider look at what different teas can and cannot do, see our guide to [the health benefits of different types of tea](https://teastory.blog/2024/05/13/the-health-benefits-of-different-types-of-tea/).

## Does Top Tea reduce weight?

Not by itself. Weight loss is the most overstated of all the benefits of Top Tea you will read about online.

No study has tested Top Tea for weight loss. The best evidence on tea and weight is for green tea, which has been studied far more than black tea. A Cochrane review of 15 trials in overweight and obese adults found that green tea preparations gave only a small weight loss that was not statistically significant and "not likely to be clinically important" ([Cochrane review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23235664/)). Black tea has even less evidence.

Where Top Tea can help is as a swap. A cup of plain tea has almost no calories. A cup with two heaped teaspoons of sugar and a few spoons of sweetened milk can carry well over 100 calories. If you replace soft drinks, malt drinks or very sweet tea with lightly sweetened Top Tea, you cut sugar, and that can help over time.

| How you take Top Tea | What it means for weight |
|-|-|
| Plain, or with a slice of lemon | Almost no calories |
| With a little milk, no sugar | A small number of calories |
| With milk and two or more teaspoons of sugar, several times a day | Adds up fast and works against weight loss |

## What Top Tea does to the body when you drink too much

The same compounds that give black tea its benefits can cause problems in large amounts or for some people.

* **Poor sleep and jitters.** Caffeine late in the day can keep you awake. Switch to herbal tea after mid afternoon if sleep is a problem.
* **Nausea on an empty stomach.** Strong black tea first thing in the morning upsets some stomachs. Our guide on [why tea can make you sick on an empty stomach](https://teastory.blog/2024/04/25/vomiting-after-tea-on-an-empty-stomach/) explains why and what helps.
* **Less iron absorbed from food.** Tea polyphenols reduce how much iron your body takes from plant foods such as beans and leafy vegetables. If you have low iron or anaemia, drink tea between meals rather than with them.
* **Tooth staining.** Dark tea stains teeth over time. Rinsing with water after your cup helps.
* **Acidity and heartburn.** Black tea is mildly acidic and caffeine can trigger reflux in some people. Read more in [is tea acidic?](https://teastory.blog/2025/10/12/is-tea-acidic-understanding-ph-levels-and-health-effects/)

## How to get the most benefits of Top Tea from each cup

1. Use one tagged bag per cup, or one round bag for two cups.
2. Pour on freshly boiled water and steep for 2 to 4 minutes. Longer gives more caffeine and more bitterness.
3. Add milk if you like, but go light on sugar.
4. Try it cold: the brand suggests serving it over ice with fruit juice.

## FAQ

### Does Top Tea contain caffeine?

Yes. Top Tea is black tea, and the brand states each cup has under 50 mg of caffeine. That is about half the caffeine of a cup of brewed coffee.

### Does Top Tea Ginger have caffeine?

Yes. The Ginger and Lemon and Lime flavours are made with the same black tea as the Classic, so they contain caffeine too.

### What are the main benefits of Top Tea?

The main benefits of Top Tea are a gentle caffeine lift, antioxidant flavonoids, a little theanine and almost calorie free fluid. Large studies link regular black tea drinking with lower heart disease risk, though they cannot prove cause.

### What does Top Tea do to the body?

The caffeine makes you more alert, the flavonoids act as antioxidants, and the water hydrates you. Too much can disturb sleep, upset an empty stomach and reduce iron absorption from meals.

### Does Top Tea reduce weight?

Not on its own. There is no good evidence that black tea burns fat. It helps only if it replaces sugary drinks and you keep the added sugar low.

### Is it safe to drink Top Tea every day?

For most healthy adults, a few cups a day is fine and well under the 400 mg caffeine limit. If you are pregnant, have reflux, low iron or a heart rhythm problem, check with your doctor about how much suits you.

## Bottom line

The benefits of Top Tea are real but modest: it is a good everyday black tea with under 50 mg of caffeine a cup, antioxidants and almost no calories when taken plain. It is not a weight loss tea, and the milk and sugar you add decide whether it helps or hurts your waistline. Enjoy a few cups a day, keep it away from iron rich meals if your iron is low, and switch to herbal tea in the evening.
