## The rewritten article (ready to paste into WordPress)

Lee MF Rhino herbal tea is a Chinese style herbal blend sold in Ghana, Nigeria and the Philippines as a male "vigor" tea. Its listed herbs, led by horny goat weed (epimedium), have very little human evidence behind them. No study shows the tea works, and Rhino branded products carry real safety warnings, so treat the benefits as marketing until proven otherwise.

That is the short version. Below you will find what the box actually lists, what each herb is known (and not known) to do, the side effects worth watching for, and the red flags that tell you a "herbal" tea may not be herbal at all.

## What is Lee MF Rhino herbal tea?

Lee MF Rhino is a boxed herbal tea in sachets, sold under the "Lee MF" brand name. Retail listings describe it as a blend of Chinese herbs made in Hong Kong and marketed for men's energy, stamina and "vitality". You will mostly find it in pharmacies, market stalls and online shops in West Africa and Southeast Asia.

It is not a true tea. There are no Camellia sinensis leaves listed, so it is not black or green tea, and the herbs on the label are not known sources of caffeine.

## Rhino tea ingredients

The Watsons Philippines product listing names these herbs. The list on that page looks cut off, so check your own box for the full panel.

| Ingredient on the label | Common name | What it is traditionally used for | What the evidence says |
|---|---|---|---|
| Herba Epimedii | Horny goat weed, yin yang huo | Traditional Chinese remedy for low libido, tiredness and weak bones | Contains icariin. Most studies are in animals or test tubes; good human trials are lacking |
| Herba Cynomorii | Cynomorium, suo yang | Used in Chinese medicine as a "kidney yang" tonic | Almost no human research |
| Rhizoma Cibotii | Cibotium fern rhizome, gou ji | Used for back pain and stiff joints | Almost no human research |

Two things stand out. First, none of these herbs has solid proof of a fast, noticeable effect in people. Second, the old claims you may see online that Rhino tea contains ginseng, maca or green tea extract do not match the retailer listing.

## Rhino tea benefits: what is claimed vs what is proven

The packaging and sellers talk about energy, stamina, circulation, kidney strength, bone health and male vigor. Here is how that stacks up.

* **Energy and tiredness:** Horny goat weed has a long history in Chinese medicine for fatigue, but there are no good human trials showing a tea like this lifts energy.
* **Circulation and blood pressure:** Lab studies suggest icariin can relax blood vessels. That has not been shown to translate into a measurable benefit from drinking a tea.
* **Bones and joints:** Epimedium and cibotium are both used traditionally for bones and back pain. Again, the evidence is mostly from animals.
* **Hydration:** It is a cup of hot water, so it adds to your fluid intake like any herbal tea. If you are curious about that side, read [does Lee MF Rhino tea dehydrate you](https://teastory.blog/2025/10/26/does-lee-mf-rhino-herbal-tea-dehydrate-or-hydrate-you/).

We are not going to make performance claims for this tea, because there is no reliable evidence to support them. If you are dealing with erectile problems, low energy or low libido, a doctor can check for real causes such as blood pressure, diabetes, low testosterone or medication side effects, and offer treatments that are tested and dosed properly.

## Rhino tea for men and for women

Rhino tea is marketed almost entirely at men. There is nothing on the label that makes it unsafe only for women, but there is also no evidence of any benefit for women, and its herbs have not been tested in pregnancy or while breastfeeding. If you are pregnant, trying to conceive or breastfeeding, skip it.

## Rhino tea side effects

Because the herbs are poorly studied, the side effect picture is incomplete. Here is what is reported for the main ingredient and for the product category.

* **Horny goat weed:** in large amounts or with long use it has been linked to dizziness, dry mouth, thirst, nosebleeds, vomiting and a racing heartbeat. It may lower blood pressure and may interact with blood thinners and blood pressure medicines.
* **Stomach upset:** bloating, nausea or loose stools are common complaints with strong herbal decoctions.
* **Headache, flushing, blocked nose, a sudden drop in blood pressure or a long lasting erection:** these are classic side effects of prescription erectile dysfunction drugs. If a "herbal" tea gives you any of them, stop drinking it and get medical advice, because it may contain a hidden drug.

People who should avoid it entirely:

* Anyone taking nitrate heart medicines (such as nitroglycerin). Hidden sildenafil type drugs combined with nitrates can drop blood pressure to dangerous levels.
* Anyone on blood thinners, blood pressure medicines or with heart, liver or kidney disease.
* Pregnant or breastfeeding women, and anyone under 18.

## Is rhino tea safe? The regulator warnings you should know

We could not find a warning from FDA Ghana or NAFDAC that names Lee MF Rhino herbal tea specifically. That is not the same as a clean bill of health. Here is the wider context:

* The US FDA says it has identified more than 25 products sold under variations of the "Rhino" name that contained hidden prescription drug ingredients, usually the same as or similar to those in erectile dysfunction medicines.
* FDA Ghana publishes a list of adulterated herbal medicines and food supplements, which has included a men's "power" herbal bitters found to contain sildenafil, and in 2025 it revoked the authorisation of another herbal mixture over undeclared medicines.
* The Philippines FDA has issued warnings about men's herbal supplements that tested positive for sildenafil.

What this means for you:

1. **Check registration.** In Ghana, look for an FDA Ghana registration number on the pack. In Nigeria, look for a NAFDAC registration number. No number, no purchase.
2. **Check the regulator's site.** Search the product name on the FDA Ghana or NAFDAC website for alerts or recalls.
3. **Buy from a pharmacy, not a roadside seller or social media page,** where fakes are more common.
4. **Report reactions.** If you get ill after drinking it, tell your doctor and report it to the regulator.

## How long is the effect of rhino tea?

There is no published research on how long Lee MF Rhino tea "lasts", and the brand does not give a figure we could verify. Herbs like epimedium, if they do anything, would act gently and over weeks, not within an hour.

That gives you a useful rule of thumb. If a herbal tea produces a strong, fast effect that kicks in within about an hour and lasts for several hours, that pattern looks far more like a prescription drug than a plant tonic. Treat it as a warning sign, not a selling point.

## Rhino tea how to use

Follow the directions printed on your box. As a general guide for herbal sachet teas:

* Steep one sachet in a cup of freshly boiled water for 5 to 10 minutes.
* Start with no more than one cup a day and see how you react.
* Do not mix it with alcohol, other "male enhancement" products or prescription erectile dysfunction drugs.
* Stop if you notice dizziness, palpitations, headache or flushing.

## Lee MF Rhino herbal tea review: our verdict

As a tea, it is simply a bitter herbal infusion. As a health product, it promises far more than the evidence supports, and it sits in a product category regulators keep flagging for hidden drugs. If you still want to try it, buy a registered pack, keep to one cup, and never combine it with heart medicines. If what you are really after is better energy or sexual health, a check up with your doctor will do more for you than any sachet.

Sellers claim more energy, stamina, better circulation and male vigor. Its main herb, horny goat weed, has traditional use for these, but there is no good human evidence that the tea delivers any of them.

Reported side effects of horny goat weed include dizziness, dry mouth, nosebleeds, vomiting and a fast heartbeat. Headache, flushing or a sharp drop in blood pressure suggest a hidden drug; stop and see a doctor.

There is no reliable data. Real herbal effects, if any, build slowly. A strong effect within an hour that lasts for hours is a red flag for undeclared drug ingredients.

### What are the ingredients in rhino tea?

### Can women drink rhino tea?

### How do you use rhino tea?
Follow the box. Typically you steep one sachet in hot water for 5 to 10 minutes and drink one cup a day at most.
